# Design system, Edward's character, presence, the shell

The calm-presence design contract in code: tokens, primitives, Edward the character and his moods, truthful presence, the shell (presence bar, dock, header, sheets, palette, keys) and login. The full design document is `docs/design-system-2026-09-25.md`; read it before changing a screen.

## Design system and Edward's presence

(The UX pass of 2026-09-25/26, "calm presence". The brief: an assistant that feels elegant, refined and *trustworthy*, with Edward himself cute but minimal.)

- **The rules the code relies on**:
  - **Edward is the status bar**: one live Edward per screen, with one plain line.
  - **The animation is a claim, so it must be true**: every mood comes from a real signal. He is never "working" when nothing runs, never "fine" when his hands are down.
  - **Emerald means Edward**: his presence and his acts (his light, the "did" rows, thinking, `Button variant="edward"` for Edward-initiated actions like "Try it" / "Wake now"). The owner's own primary actions are neutral high-contrast (`variant="primary"` = `bg-text-primary text-primary-bg`), navigation highlights are neutral, and status colours never borrow emerald.
  - **Calm by default**: one loop per screen (his breath); everything else moves once, for a reason, in under a second. Show the work, then fold it into a receipt.
  - **Thumb first**: 44 pt targets, whole cards tappable, undo instead of confirm.
  - **Copy is his voice**: first person, sentence case, no internal vocabulary ("triage", "heartbeat", "pulse") on owner-facing surfaces.
  - **One clock format, "7:34 pm"**, everywhere since 2026-09-27. `lib/time.ts` (`formatClock`, which also takes a `Date` so a zone-less calendar time is not reread as UTC, and `formatDateTime`) lower-cases the day period after a plain space (`joinClockParts`) instead of Intl's "9:00 AM" with a narrow no-break space. `whenLine`, `CalendarPanel`, the /screen panels and stage (its `LiveClock` `face="stage"`, the "Said · 5:52 pm" overline — whose clock keeps its lower-case pm under the overline's capitals — "since", "Earlier ·", "On my mind · 5:10 pm", "Just thought ·", the why receipt, the pets chip, and the calendar and Listening stage faces in `stageLive.ts`), the Locale panel and the five hub clocks all use it. 24-hour clock faces (LiveClock's default face, RecentsPanel, the heartbeat time column) stay as they are. `__tests__/clockFormat.test.tsx` fails on any code that shows Intl's AM/PM.
- **Tokens** (`app/globals.css` top, `tailwind.config.ts`): every colour is a raw var plus an `-rgb` channel twin, and Tailwind maps `rgb(var(--x-rgb) / <alpha-value>)`. **Why**: the colours were `var(--x)` strings, so every alpha class (`bg-terminal/20`, `border-border/70`, `bg-primary-bg/95`: 128 uses in 35 files) compiled to nothing. Translucent tokens (`--border`, the `-soft` fills, `--glow`, `--input-border`) keep their resting alpha in `--*-alpha`, and `/NN` scales it (the config's `scaled`), so `border-border/50` is half a hairline, and bare `border` / `divide` draw the hairline instead of Tailwind's gray-200. `:root` is light, `.dark` (the default, set before paint) is dark; text keeps ≥ 4.5:1 on every surface and on its own soft pill.
  - Type: `text-overline · caption · footnote · body (15/22) · callout · title3 · title2 · title1 · display` (Inter; JetBrains Mono for data and code only, never Edward's voice; inputs ≥ 16 px against iOS zoom).
  - Radii `xs 6 · sm 8 · md 12 · lg 16 · xl 22`; shadows `e1 e2 e3` and `glow` (only while he thinks, listens or speaks).
  - Motion: `duration-press/fast/base/slow` = 90/160/240/360 ms with `ease-out` / `ease-in-out` / `ease-in`; framer springs and `REDUCED_FADE` in `components/ui/motion.ts`; reduced motion is ≤ 120 ms fades and no loops.
  - **The global `[class*="bg-"] { transition }` rule is deleted**: it overrode every transition utility on anything with a background. A theme switch cross-fades through a ~300 ms `html.theme-transition` class (`lib/theme.tsx`) and nothing else animates colour globally. **No `[class*=` selectors in global CSS.**
- **Primitives** (`components/ui`, barrel `index.ts`): `Button` (primary · edward · secondary · ghost · danger; `md` 44 on touch / 36 on md+, `sm` 36 drawn with a 44 hit area), `Card`, `Row`, `Pill`, `Segment`, `SectionLabel`, `EmptyState`, `Sheet` (framer spring, drag to dismiss, Esc, focus trap and return, scroll lock; a bottom sheet on the phone, a dialog on md+) and `ConfirmSheet`, `Switch`, `Field` / `Input` / `Textarea` / `Select` (`CONTROL`), `Notice`, `Meter`, `Skeleton`, `Pulse` (alias `EdwardPulse`: loading is Edward's breathing pip, never `Loader2 animate-spin`), `Toast` with Undo (`useToast`: 4.5 s, 7 s with Undo, at most 3, above the dock), `PanelShell` (a settings panel's frame: a breadcrumb to its room via `roomFor`, a `title1`, one line).
  - **`cn` lives in `components/ui/cn.ts`** (`lib/utils` re-exports it): `tailwind-merge` extended with the type scale, the `e*` / `glow` shadows and the duration tokens. Stock twMerge reads `text-body` as a colour, so `cn("text-body", "text-text-primary")` silently dropped the size.
  - Every `LazyMotion` loads `MOTION_FEATURES` (`components/ui/motionFeatures.ts`), framer's full `domMax` feature set: with `domAnimation` there is no drag or layout, so sheets could not be swiped down and the dock pill snapped until /chat happened to load `domMax`. The module stays apart from `motion.ts`, so a leaf import of the motion tokens never pulls framer-motion in.
  - `/login`, `AuthGuard`, `/hub` and `/screen` import primitives by leaf path, never the `components/ui` barrel (it pulls framer-motion into their first load).
- **Guardrail tests**: `__tests__/colorTokens.test.ts` (compiles the real Tailwind: alpha-token classes must be in the CSS, every colour class in app/components/lib must resolve to a configured key, no raw `zinc|gray|slate-N`, no `[class*=` in globals), `tailwindTokens.test.ts`, `tokenContrast.test.ts` (≥ 4.5:1, read from globals.css), `touchTargets.test.ts`, `utilsCn.test.ts`, `ui.test.tsx`, `motionFeatures.test.tsx`, `buildLint.test.ts`, `settingsBundle.test.ts` (the Edward tab's first load never statically imports the chat's markdown renderer; Prism was ~266 kB gzipped).

## Edward, the character

(`components/pane/PaneSvg.tsx` draws and labels him; `lib/edwardMood.ts` decides; `lib/useEdwardLife.ts` gives him life; the "Pane buddy" section of `app/globals.css` turns attributes into motion; `components/EdwardAvatar.tsx` sizes him — `sm 32 · md 64 · lg 96 · xl 120` or px, ≤ 36 px a still bust.) The app icon's palette: a cream shell, a dark visor, pill eyes and a chest light in his accent; light mode adds a faint outline.

- **Moods** (`data-mood`): `idle think work listen speak attention sleepy resting offline`, from the pure `deriveMood` in one priority: offline > speak > listen > think/work > attention (a held approval or an unread thought) > resting (the quota spent) > sleepy (23:00–06:00) > idle. Before any answer about his mind (`known: false`) he is plain idle with an empty line, never a "Resting" guess while an approval may be waiting.
- `statusLine` / `statusLabel` are the one honest line and its aria form ("Working · 3 tools", "Thinking · morning wake", "Waiting on your approval", "Wants you · 1 new thought", "Resting until Tue 3:00 pm", "Offline"; a resting or waiting line adds "· Chrome link down"). Raw wake reasons never show (`REASON_PHRASES`).
- **Beats** (`data-beat`, one-shot, within `BEAT_MS`): `nod` (a tool or turn went well), `wince` (a failure), `wave` (first open of the local day), `acted` (antenna sonar: he reached outward — a send, a push, a note), `perk` (back after ≥ 15 s away). They are ≥ 1.2 s apart, and of two held the one that matters more plays (acted > wince > wave > nod > perk).
- **Life**: `useEdwardLife` writes `data-blink` / `data-glance` / `data-beat` straight onto the DOM (a blink costs zero React renders): blinks every 2.2–6 s (15 % double, 12 % a long stare, slower sleepy or resting, none offline), idle glances every 8–20 s unless a `gaze` is set, everything paused while the tab is hidden and off under reduced motion.
- **Pinned geometry** (tests pin it; the hub depends on it): viewBox `0 0 36 38`, foot pads' bottom at y = 34 (`hub/lib/walkEngine.ts` grounds the companion on `PANE_FOOT_Y`), the antenna path `M18 6.5 Q18 3 17 1.5`, every pivot in viewBox px with the default transform-box (never `fill-box`), the classes `hub.css` / `display.css` drive kept on the same parts. The `bust` variant crops with an outer viewBox around the same nested canvas; legacy `act-*` activity classes read as moods. Tests: `edwardMood.test.ts`, `useEdwardLife.test.tsx`, `paneSvg.test.tsx`, `paneCss.test.ts`, `paneTransformOrigin.test.ts`, `edwardAvatar.test.tsx`.
- **One live Edward per screen; the antenna tip is his trust light.** Only `.is-live` breathes (5.2 s, rising faster than he falls) and runs mood loops; every other Edward is a still pose until a beat, and nothing loops inside a scrolling list. The shell's presence bar (phone) or header (desktop) carries the live one. A page with its own hero (Today's hero, chat's empty state, the Edward tab's hero, the voice view) registers it (`usePresenceHero(ref)` for a hero that can scroll away, `useRegisterPresenceHero(open)` for one that covers the screen; `lib/presenceHero.tsx`, a module store, "on screen" at ≥ 40 % visible), and the bar fades its own Edward while the hero shows, like an iOS large title. The presence sheet has its own live one, so nothing else breathes while it is open. `useFreshBeat` never replays an old beat on mount or unfold.
- The tip: a steady accent at rest, a slow pulse thinking, amber for `attention` and `resting` **and whenever `data-hands="down"`** (his Chrome link is dead: never a calm emerald; offline keeps grey), a sonar ping on `acted`, grey offline. With Chrome hands turned off (`EDWARD_CHROME_HANDS`, the default) the link is unknown, not down, so the tip is not amber for it ([screen-and-browser.md](screen-and-browser.md)).

## Presence is truthful

(`lib/presence.tsx` `EdwardPresenceProvider`; every decision pure in `lib/presenceModel.ts`; `lib/mindNowApi.ts` normalises any body.)

- The signals are the chat turn (`useChatPresence`), live voice, the network, and `GET /api/mind/now`: polled on mount, every 20 s while visible, at once when the tab returns, never while hidden. A failed poll retries after 5 s, then backs off to 5 min, and he reads offline only after two failures in a row.
- Every answer is cached (`localStorage` `edward:mindNow:v1`), and a cold open paints one under 10 minutes old; with none, he makes no claim until the first answer.
- Beats come from real transitions only (`turnBeat`: a tool ok or failed, a turn ending, an outward act; `mindBeat`: mind/now's last wake changed to one that acted; perk; the day's first wave).
- `useEdwardPresence()` → `{signals, mood, status, label, beat, mindNow, online, refresh}`.
- The polite live region says his mood, never a tool count (one turn once read out 26 phrases), and holds still through a chat turn; the chat's own `TurnAnnouncer` says the turn ("Thinking", "Working", "Writing", then "Edward replied: …").
- Tests: `presence.test.tsx`, `presenceModel.test.ts`, `presenceHeroScroll.test.tsx`, `mindNowApi.test.ts`, `turnAnnouncer.test.tsx`.

## The shell

(`components/layout/ClientLayout.tsx`.) Only the chrome for the current size is mounted: hidden with CSS, the desktop header and chat sidebar re-rendered on every streamed token on the phone.

- **Phone**: `PresenceBar` (48 px plus the safe area, translucent: the live bust, his name and line; the right slot is history and new chat on chat, search elsewhere — `barActions`), the page, and `ThumbDock` (Today · Chat · Edward, 56 px drawn plus `max(env(safe-area-inset-bottom), 8px)`, a sliding pill; the Edward tab is his still bust with an emerald pip while he wants the owner — `edwardTabBadge`; tapping the tab you are on scrolls to the top, pops a sub-page to its root, or on Chat starts a new chat — `dockTapAction`).
- **Desktop (md+)**: `DesktopHeader` (presence left, a segmented `Today · Chat · Edward` centre, Search ⌘K and `AvatarMenu` — appearance, Edward's screen, sign out behind `SignOutSheet` — right).
- **Overlays mount once**:
  - **`PresenceSheet`** (tap Edward anywhere): Right now, Waiting on you, What I did (his last five wakes, with "Read my thinking →"), Running on (the next wake, the quota, the Chrome link). "Wake him now" posts the 202 wake and shows "Waking…" until the poll sees it; offline, it claims only what is known; plain words from `presenceSheetModel.ts`.
  - **`CommandPalette`** (⌘K, or the phone bar's search): recent conversations and actions, then server-searched conversations, goals, settings and actions as the owner types (`commandPaletteModel.ts`).
  - **`KeyboardLayer`** (the keymap pure in `lib/hotkeys.ts`): ⌘K the palette even while typing, `/` the composer, Esc closes the top sheet or else stops the turn, ⌘⇧O a new chat, ⌘\ the chat sidebar, `g t` · `g c` · `g e`. While typing only Esc, ⌘K and the two modifier combinations count; while a dialog is open only Esc and ⌘K. ⌘ on a Mac, Ctrl elsewhere. `edward:focus-composer` / `edward:stop-turn` are cancelable window events a page handles first.
- **The bottom-inset contract**: only the layout owns it. The dock pads the home indicator while shown, and the shell sets `--shell-inset-bottom` (0 with the dock, the inset while the dock folds away for typing; `lib/chrome.ts::shellBottomInset`) for the composer to pad by, never both. The dock publishes `--dock-h` (56, 0 when hidden or on /login), so toasts and the notification prompt (which publishes `--prompt-h`; under the bar on chat, above the dock elsewhere, hidden while typing — `promptPlacement`) sit above it.
- **Theme**: `lib/themeMode.ts` (light · dark · system, following the OS), `lib/themeColors.ts` (`theme-color` = the canvas per theme, set before paint and on toggle).
- Tests: `shell.test.tsx`, `shellBeats.test.tsx`, `shellPages.test.tsx`, `clientLayout.test.tsx`, `presenceSheetModel.test.ts`, `commandPaletteModel.test.ts`, `hotkeys.test.ts`, `chrome.test.ts`, `navItems.test.ts`, `theme.test.tsx`.

## Login

(`app/(auth)/login/page.tsx`, pure `lib/loginFlow.ts`.) Edward at the door is the one live Edward. He looks away while a hidden password is typed and peeks when it is shown (`loginGaze`), winces at a wrong one, and nods at the right one before the page fades (540 + 360 ms) to `?redirect=` or `/today`. The greeting names the owner when `EDWARD_OWNER_NAME` is set ("Welcome back, Sam."), else "Welcome back."; the avatar shows the name's initial, else a person glyph. Errors are his voice (`loginErrorCopy`: "I can't reach the Mac right now", too many tries, "That's not it"). A hidden `username` field serves password managers. The auth rules themselves are in [auth-and-pwa.md](auth-and-pwa.md).
