# The household hub

A shared touchscreen in the home (the reference build used a Raspberry Pi at the pet station): a thin client on `/hub`, its pure logic and tests, the household and its pets, the design rules that must not be improved away, and its gotchas.

## The hub

**A second device for the household**: a Raspberry Pi 4 with a 7" touch display, running Chromium in kiosk mode against this app's `/hub`. It is a thin client: no Edward code runs on it, and all the logic stays on the host Mac. Any browser in kiosk mode on `/hub` works; setup notes for the Pi are in [pi-hub-setup.md](../pi-hub-setup.md). Over plain-HTTP LAN the kiosk needs `--unsafely-treat-insecure-origin-as-secure=http://my-mac.local:3000` (your host's name), or `getUserMedia` (the mic) and the Wake Lock API silently fail.

(`backend/services/hub_*.py`, `backend/routers/hub.py`, `frontend/app/(display)/hub/`)

- **Sibling of `/display`, not a fork**: `/screen` (with its `/display` alias) is Edward's own stage on the host Mac's monitor ([screen-and-browser.md](screen-and-browser.md)); `/hub` is the household's control panel.
- **The household** comes from `EDWARD_HOUSEHOLD`: comma-separated names, owner first (for example `Sam,Rowan`; unset, just the owner, or "Me" when no `EDWARD_OWNER_NAME` is set). `GET /api/hub/who` serves it as `people` (`hub_who_service.hub_people()`, read per call), the panel remembers the list, and its who-toggle is drawn from it (hidden with fewer than two people; before the server answers, nobody is selected).
- **Themes go by position, not by name** (`hub/lib/whoTheme.ts`): the first person gets the `first` palette, the second `second`, the third `third` (silver/graphite); anyone past the last palette, or not in the household (a pet sitter), gets the first. Only the identity accent changes (rule 3 below).
- **Who is at the hub** (2026-09-24): the hub posts a change of person (`POST /api/hub/who`, from `useWho`), and doses and list edits refresh it (`touch_hub_person`). It is stored as the `display_config` row `hub_who` and cleared after 30 minutes idle (`hub_who_service.current_hub_person`), while the panel's own toggle stays sticky: a stale name corrected with one tap beats blocking a 6 am log. `hub_service.hub_presence_line()` ("At the hub: Rowan (since 10:02)") opens the mind's pack and the pulse's trigger. Tests: `tests/test_hub_who.py`, `__tests__/whoStore.test.ts`, `__tests__/hubHousehold.public.test.ts`.
- **The pets and their care come from a seed file** (`EDWARD_HUB_SEED`, a JSON path; relative paths are read from `backend/`). Copy `backend/hub_seed.example.json` (a fictional dog and two cats), describe your own pets, and set the variable. On the first board read with no pets in the database, `hub_service.seed_if_empty` plants it once; unset, the board starts empty and nothing alerts. Each pet gets a windowed food bowl per entry in `food`; `care` lists one slot per dose (`timed` with an `at_time`, `window`, or `prn`), `needs_review` marks something unconfirmed, and `bundle_with` lets one tap on a bowl log a dose given with it (in the example, the evening "Probiotic powder" rides on Pepper's pm bowl, which shows a `+P` badge for it: `+` and the name's first letter, or a last word of one or two characters, so "Vitamin E" is `+E`). Editing the file later changes nothing: clear the `pets` and `care_items` rows first. Test: `tests/test_public_hub_seed.py`.
- **The care day is in the owner's timezone** from settings (`hub_pet_service.household_tz()`, read per call, so a settings change applies at once). Today's "Log given" action records the first household member as `given_by`.
- **Voice at the hub is not the owner** (2026-10-11): `/hub/ask` mounts the app's live voice with `surface="hub"`, so its turns run as `hub_voice`: someone in the owner's home, not necessarily the owner. It texts only through the recipient rule and never to the owner (`tell_owner` reaches them as the reach origin `hub`: counted, a note overnight, said once — [push-and-notes.md](push-and-notes.md)); stand-downs and the Chrome typing rule hold; on `EDWARD_TOOLSET=legacy` it has no sends or shell at all. It spends Edward's own share, not the owner's reserve; what is said there is never the owner's own word in memory ([memory.md](memory.md)); a result that finishes after the session ends is a note, not a push. Before this the panel ran as the owner's own `voice`, so anyone at the panel had the owner's rights ([voice.md](voice.md)).
- Pure logic is split from I/O, and that is where the tests live:
  - `hub_pet_service.py`: the care-day boundary, board state, overdue/missed
  - `hub_list_service.py`: list ordering and self-clearing
  - `hub_health_service.py`: the watchdog assessment, disk and backup status, the rollup
  - `hub_settings_service.py`: screensaver settings validation
  - `hub_photos_service.py`: photo listing, path safety
  - `hub_service.py`: the database layer for all of the above, and the seed
- Frontend pure modules under `hub/lib/` (`slideshow.ts`, `walkEngine.ts`, `clockInk.ts`, `photoLayout.ts`, `whoTheme.ts`, `whoStore.ts`) are unit-tested in `frontend/__tests__/`.
- **Hub health** (`hub_health_service.py`, `GET /api/hub/health`): the LaunchAgent rows are named from `EDWARD_LAUNCHD_PREFIX` (default `com.meet-edward`), a tunnel row appears only when `EDWARD_TUNNEL_LABEL` is set, and the backup folder is `EDWARD_BACKUP_DIR` (default `~/edward-backups`; a missing one reads "backup folder missing (…)").

**Design rules that must not be "improved" away:**

1. **Alert fatigue is the top risk.** `schedule_type="window"` items (a cat's wet food, given "twice daily, whenever") NEVER go overdue. Only `timed` items (a dog's 06:00/18:00 medication, say) alert. If free-fed food could raise an amber badge, the colour would be meaningless within a week, and then ignored when a real dose is late.
2. **`missed` (more than 4 h late) stops alerting** but is still recorded. An alert nobody can act on trains people to ignore alerts; whether to catch up a long-late dose is a conversation with the vet, not a badge.
3. **Status colours are fixed across user themes.** Per-person theming swaps the identity palette only; `--hub-info` and the given/late/missed/refused ramp never move. "Overdue" must mean the same thing whoever is at the screen.
4. **A photo in a visible slideshow slot is never mutated.** New photos go into the hidden slot, then visibility flips. Deriving both layers from one index is what made slides glitch.
5. **`needs_review` items render with a badge** rather than looking authoritative. A medication schedule copied from a pet-sitter's note, with some drug names or strengths unconfirmed, must look unconfirmed until a person confirms it.

**The late-dose alert, in code** (`backend/services/care_watch.py`, 2026-10-11). The judgement check's `late_dose` scenario used to ask a model, in the evening wake, to notice an unlogged pill. A missed dose is real harm, so the check is code now. It runs outside `EDWARD_PROACTIVITY` and the spend cap (it makes no model call), on every `*/5` `proactive_sweep` ([scheduler.md](scheduler.md)).

- **When it alerts.** A timed item still `overdue` at due + 45 min (`CARE_LATE_MINUTES`) pushes the owner once. Rules 1 and 2 hold: a `window` item never goes overdue, so it never alerts, and a `missed` dose (more than 240 min) stops alerting.
- **One push per pet and slot**, not per drug: "Biscuit's 6:00 am Carprofen and Joint chew aren't logged — 47 min late. Given? Log them on the hub." The text adds "(schedule unconfirmed)" when an item `needs_review` (rule 5). It links to `/hub/pets`.
- **When it holds back.** Someone who touched the hub in the last 15 minutes (`hub_service.get_hub_person`'s `last_seen`) is standing at the station and sees the alert there.
- **Delivery.** `reach_service.tell_owner(..., urgent=True, origin="care", dedupe_key="care:<pet>:<care day>:<HH:MM>")`, exempt from the push budget and from quiet hours: a 06:45 alert is about a real dose.
- **No repeats, and no silent failures.** Each alert is a keyed `care_alert` ledger row, and only a push that landed (`delivery_outcome` → `pushed`) stops the alerting, so a restart or the next sweep never re-alerts. A push that did not land (the transport failed, or it reached no device) is not a push. Review 2026-10-11: "Left it as a note — the push failed" used to count as pushed, so an evening dose lived only on Today and was never retried. Now `reach_service` leaves the owner a note (`downgraded`), and every sweep tries the push again with `note_fallback=False` (no second note; `failed` while it keeps failing) until it lands or the dose is logged or missed. A transport that raises is `failed` and retried the same way. It also writes a `noticings` row (`origin='care'`).
- **Kill switch.** `EDWARD_CARE_WATCH=off` (for a household with no timed doses).

Tests: `tests/test_care_watch.py`.

## The household photo door

`/photos` is a small PWA for someone else in the household (2026-10-11), with its own password, that reaches only the hub's photo album and its screensaver settings: the same files and the same settings row the hub reads ([auth-and-pwa.md](auth-and-pwa.md)).

- **Off unless configured**: `PHOTO_DOOR_PASSWORD_HASH` holds a bcrypt hash of the door's password (single-quoted in `.env`, so its `$`s stay as written whichever reader parses the file). Unset or malformed, a login is refused (403), the other door routes answer 404, and the door's auth status says `enabled: false`.
- The API lives under `/api/photo-door/*` (`routers/photo_door.py`, `services/photo_door_auth.py`); its session is a JWT with subject `photo_door`, and the owner's own session opens it too. Settings it changes are recorded as `updated_by="photo_door"`.
- On the door someone can add photos, turn them (`hub_photos_service.rotate_photo`: `sips -r`, JPEG/PNG only; it writes `<name>-rotated.jpg` and removes the old file, never re-encoding a name in place — rule 4, and the kiosk caches a name for 24 h), delete them (two taps), and set the screensaver. Its grid loads `sips` thumbnails (480 px) cached outside the album in `~/Library/Caches/edward-photo-thumbs` (`EDWARD_PHOTO_THUMBS_DIR`), keyed by name and mtime.

## Gotchas

- Photos live in `~/edward-photos` **on the host Mac** (`EDWARD_PHOTOS_DIR`). HEIC is deliberately excluded from *display*: Chromium can't draw it and iPhones default to it, so the count of skipped files is shown in the UI. Convert existing ones with `sips -s format jpeg -Z 1920`.
- **Uploads** come in through Settings → Hub Photos (`HubPhotosPanel`), so the album can be filled from a phone rather than over SSH. `POST /api/hub/photos` accepts HEIC and converts it to JPEG with `sips` on the way in (`hub_photos_service.save_upload`); the name is reduced to a safe leaf, a collision gets a `-2` suffix rather than overwriting, and a served photo carries `X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff` because the directory is browser-writable.
- **The screensaver companion is positioned by his feet, not his box.** PaneSvg's viewBox is `0 0 36 38` inside a square element and his soles stop at y=34, so the bottom ~10 % is empty air; `groundedBottomPx()` in `walkEngine.ts` subtracts it, and `.hub-companion` sets `line-height: 0` to kill the inline-flex baseline gap underneath. Position him by the box again and he hovers.
- He walks at 16 ms/px (`WALK_MS_PER_PX`; at 11 his feet skated) with short ramps (`easeWalk`: 15 % speeding up and slowing down, peak ≈ 1.18× his average; the quadratic ease peaked at 2×). The walk writes `trackTransform(x)` to his track element every frame and commits `x` to React state only on arrival: setting state per frame re-rendered the companion ~60 times a second on the Pi. Under reduced motion he fades out and back in instead (`planMove`).
- The greeting card has the hub's live Edward (`GreetingEdward`: mood from the same presence as /screen; he waves on the day's first show and when someone new taps in at the who-toggle, never on a reload). Tests: `__tests__/walkEngine.test.ts`, `walkGrounded.test.ts`, `photoCompanion.test.tsx`, `hubGreetingEdward.test.tsx`.
- **In slide mode he shoves the photo the way it leaves** (2026-09-27): the slide transition sends the photo off to the left (`translateX(-100%)`) while he used to face right. The screensaver passes `pushDirection(settings.transition)`; pushing left he faces left (the lean and arms mirror inside the flipped svg) and his step runs mirrored keyframes (`hub-push-step-left`), stilled under reduced motion. Test: `__tests__/photoCompanion.test.tsx`.
- `usePoll` pauses on hidden tabs by design. Poll-driven panels therefore render empty in browser-automation screenshots while one-shot `useEffect` fetches populate normally: an artifact, not a bug.
- **The hub uses the normal password.** A scoped device token plus a PIN gate for sensitive screens is designed but **not built**, so skill toggles are deliberately kept off the hub. `/hub/settings` says this on screen, so a missing "Restart Edward" button reads as a decision rather than an oversight: that button would give a shared-password wall panel the power to run commands on the host Mac.
- **`/hub/settings` is about the panel, not about Edward.** Sleep (→ screensaver), Screen off (a black page with the wake lock released so the display can actually sleep; a tap wakes to `/hub`, not back to settings, since whoever wakes it usually isn't whoever blacked it), Reload hub (`location.reload()`, the fix for a wedged kiosk), Exit hub (→ `/chat`, two taps). The two-tap confirm **expires after 6 s** (`deviceActions.ts`): an armed button left in front of the next person would turn their first innocent tap into an action they never started. `expire()` clears the record rather than letting `isArmed` merely answer false; otherwise the countdown timer never stops and the kiosk re-renders twice a second forever.
- **New builds**: the hub reloads onto a new frontend build by itself when nobody has touched the screen for 30 s (the `(display)` layout's `useBuildReload`), except while "Screen off" is on (a reload would light the panel back up for the night) or while live voice on `/hub/ask` has the mic open. A Hub Photos upload holds the Settings tab it runs in, not the kiosk ([app-shell-today-chat.md](app-shell-today-chat.md#new-builds-reach-open-tabs)).
- **Is the kiosk alive?** With `EDWARD_HEALTH_HUB_KIOSK` set, the deep health check reports the kiosk silent after 6 h without one of its own polls (board, canvas, who, lists, settings, the screensaver's `photos?seed=`); a phone's `/api/hub/*` calls don't count. On 2026-09-27 the reference kiosk had been dark for 36 hours with nothing noticing ([ops-health-backups.md](ops-health-backups.md#health-watchdog)).
- Every hub clock says "7:34 pm" (`lib/time`), like the rest of the app.

## Adapting this

The hub is laid out for one device, a 1280×720 touchscreen kiosk (`HubRoot.tsx`); the pets come from the seed file (so the late-dose alert has nothing to watch until it lists timed doses), and the kiosk health check and the photo door are off until configured. Each of these is marked `ADAPT:` in the code; [ADAPTING.md](../ADAPTING.md) lists them with what to change.
