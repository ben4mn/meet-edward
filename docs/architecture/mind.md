# The mind: wakes, the pack, the journal

Edward thinking when nobody is talking to him: `mind_service.wake(reason)`, what the pack carries, follow-through on promises, the silent nightly, free time, the heartbeat's mind path, the state document, standing instructions, the thought journal, retries, the judgement check. The mind was rebuilt on 2026-09-18 (one wake primitive over a raw pack, replacing thousands of lines of scored packs, ranking and suppression), and proactivity was rebuilt as noticing, not a schedule, on 2026-10-11. The noticer, the speak gate, the speaker, `reach_service.tell_owner` and the late-dose alert are in [noticing.md](noticing.md) and [push-and-notes.md](push-and-notes.md); pings — the path that answers anyone who asks Edward directly — in [pings.md](pings.md).

## Proactivity is noticing, not a schedule (2026-10-11)

(`backend/services/mind_service.py`, `services/scheduled_events_service.py`, `services/wake_state.py`, `services/judgement_eval.py`)
- **The decision.** Three reviewers scored four designs; the one built is "notice, then speak": a code floor first (it ships whatever else happens), then a cheap noticer and a verified speaker behind `EDWARD_PROACTIVITY` (`off` by default, then `shadow`, then `notice` only when the real-model evals pass). **What it overturned** (keeping one budgeted free-time run a day): free time as the surplus queue ran it, a stage "always doing something", the 07:00 and 18:00 ticks with a morning family brief, the Sunday 09:00 reflect, the full-pack `messages` wake, a daily memory push, and goals and ideas in the mind's pack. Why, from the original install's evidence: 0 of 33 pushes were opened; the best-received act was a promise kept in the chat where it was made; about $7.4 a day on Claude went mostly to `messages` wakes that relayed texts the owner had already read.
- **What can wake the mind now**: a `self` wake he scheduled (a promise's follow-up), a `mention` (an iMessage `@edward`, only while pings are off — mail and calendar rows never are, below), the 03:00 `nightly`, the surfaces pulse (`surfaces`), a `speak` turn for something the noticer found (dark until the flag), the 13:00 `free` run, and an operator's `manual` (`POST /api/mind/wake`). `mind_service.REASONS` is exactly `manual, self, mention, surfaces, speak, nightly, free, messages`; `messages` is only a shim (below), and the router refuses anything else.
- **Every wake runs under a policy** (`TurnPolicy`: `source`, `owner_asked`, `may_reach_people`, `may_push`, `reply_to`, `deadline_s` — [tool-loop.md](tool-loop.md)). `wake(reason, *, policy=None, event_meta=None, timeout_s=None, …)`: a caller's policy wins, else `policy_values(reason, …)` derives one — `mention` may reach the chats it was asked in (`reply_to` from its mentions' `chat_identifier`) and push; `manual` and a plain `self` may tell the owner but message nobody; `nightly` and `surfaces` reach nobody; `speak` with no policy is fail-closed. `make_policy` builds the class and passes only the fields it has. `deadline_s` is the wake's timeout minus 30 s. For `EDWARD_TOOLSET=legacy`, where the old tools check no policy, the legacy `allowed_tools` always ride along: `LEGACY_WAKE_TOOLS` narrowed by the policy (`legacy_tools_for`: no sends without `may_reach_people`, no push tool without `may_push`), `LEGACY_QUIET_TOOLS` for the nightly, `legacy_speak_tools(channel)` for the speaker, `LEGACY_FREE_TOOLS` for free time. A wake is never given `forget_about`.
- **Threads, timeouts, windows**: `edward-mind` (240 s, 24K) for `manual` and `self`; a mention 420 s; `edward-speak` ("Edward's noticing", 180 s — 90 s when the item closes within half an hour — and no history: the speak pack carries what was said today); `edward-nightly` ("Edward's nights", 600 s, 900 s on Sunday, 48K); `edward-free-time` ("Edward's free time", 480 s, 12K). `EDWARD_OWN_THREAD_IDS` holds these plus `edward-surfaces` and the retired `edward-research` (old rows live there). Escalations are served to a `mention` only (`serves_escalations(reason)`); every other wake neither sees nor stamps them.
- **Inbound is noticed, never woken for** (the shim): `request_wake("messages")` — still called by `mind_path` — and `wake("messages")` hand inbound to `noticing_service.request_notice(None)`; when that fails, `store_unseen_inbound` stamps the escalated, unseen events of the last 24 h `notification_batch_id="stored:<pass>"` and writes one `ledger_events` row (`kind="notice"`, `source="stub"`), so Today's "waiting on you" cannot flood. It never stamps a ping (`triage_status` ping or `raw_data.ping`), the owner asking (`owner_direct`) or an iMessage `@edward` (`MENTION_RE`, the same pattern as `triage_service.MENTION_PATTERN`): someone is waiting on those, and with pings off a mention wake answers them (`storable_inbound`); an email or calendar row with `@edward` in it is stored like the rest. `REARM_REASONS` is `mention` alone; a mention follow-up whose mentions are handled hands the rest to the shim.
- **Follow-through: a promise made in a ping is kept in that chat.** While the ping runner answers a ping, `services.pings.hooks.CURRENT_PING` is set; `scheduled_events_service.create_event` stamps `event_metadata.ping = {ping_id, chat_identifier, chat_guid, is_group, conversation_id}` beside `created_in` (`ping_stamp`; an existing stamp is kept). When the event fires the scheduler calls `wake("self", extra=why, event_meta=meta)`; `follow_through_for(meta)` finds the chat from the stamp, else from the intention's `source_ref` (`turn_source` `ping` or `ping_owner`, with `chat_identifier`), and the wake may reply there (`may_reach_people`, `reply_to` = every spelling of the chat; `owner_asked` from the `requested_by_owner` stamp; `final_text_is_reply=True`, so a look that finds nothing ends with its one line and is never nudged by the reply check into posting "nothing yet" into the group — only a mention is nudged). Its pack leads with a **Follow-through** block (`follow_through_context`, `follow_through_lines`): the chat, the promise, who asked and when (the pings row), the chat's last 20 lines with Edward's own labelled from the reader's `by_edward`, the chat's ping lines, and the ask — reply there when there is something or the promise is due; otherwise say so in one line and `schedule` the next look or close the intention. While a ping turn is running in that chat (`pings.hooks.chats_in_flight()`) the wake does not run: it re-arms itself as a new `mind_wake` event 10 minutes on (`rearmed` counts; after six it runs anyway). Retries follow `should_retry_wake`: never after the wake reached someone or committed. Test: `tests/test_follow_through.py`.
- **The nightly: 03:00, silent, skipped when nothing happened.** It replaces the surplus queue's `reflect_lite` and the Sunday 09:00 reflect (the scheduler seeds `wakeup_tick` `nightly` at `0 3 * * *` in the owner's zone). A code pre-check first, `nightly_has_material`: since the last nightly row (or 24 h) — a noticing, a ping, a verdict (`mind_verdict`/`feedback` signals, heartbeat feedback), an intention whose horizon lapsed, a turn in which the owner spoke (`memory_service.OWNER_SPEAKS_SOURCES`), or a new heard/owner memory. Nothing → one `mind` row (`source="nightly"`, `left_alone`, "nothing new since last night") and no model call; a check that cannot run says yes. Otherwise `wake("nightly")` in `edward-nightly` under a policy that reaches nobody, with the day's asks (close intentions, what he learned, the state document only if something changed) or, on Sunday in the owner's zone (`is_sunday`), the week's (`collect_week`) and the rewrite of the state document; then the noticing digest code wrote (`noticing_service.digest(now)`), read-only. The pack says nobody is awake: never a push or a message, at most one note. After the turn: Sunday's decay pass (`decay_stale_memories(dry_run=False)`, its stamp in `detail.decay` — it also runs on an empty or quota-skipped Sunday; [memory.md](memory.md)), contradiction triage by note with at most one question (`contradiction_triage_service.triage_unresolved(limit=5, priority="background", purpose="mind:nightly", ask_via="note", max_asks=1)`, in the background on its own 240 s bound). The surfaces pulse for the morning scene is the scheduler's nightly tick's, once, after the wake returns (`pulse_in_background("nightly", extra=NIGHTLY_PULSE_EXTRA)`, [scheduler.md](scheduler.md)); the wake never pulses itself (both once did, two surfaces turns a night). A caller that brings its own pack (the judgement check) gets neither the pre-check nor the after-steps. `RETRY_REASONS` is `self`, `mention`, `nightly`. Test: `tests/test_nightly.py`.
- **Free time, one budgeted run a day** (design decision, 2026-10-11: one run a day of about $0.30–0.50 for the stage and his own projects — no push, no send). A `wakeup_tick` `free` at 13:00 in the owner's zone (`scheduler_service.FREE_CRON`) runs `wake("free")` in `edward-free-time` (`FREE_CONVERSATION_ID`, `thread_for`), 480 s (`FREE_TIMEOUT_SECONDS`), a 12K history (`FREE_MAX_HISTORY_CHARS`), the raw pack plus `free_lines()` (the choice is his; leave what he makes in a document or a memory; no push, no message, no note; this Mac's screen is the one surface, shown never spoken; the messages are context). Its policy reaches nobody and never pushes (`_REACH_BY_REASON`), with a per-run cap of $0.30 (`FREE_MAX_TURN_USD` → `TurnPolicy.max_turn_usd`) and at most 8,000 output tokens a call, the closing call included (`FREE_MAX_OUTPUT_TOKENS` → `TurnPolicy.max_output_tokens`): the dollar cap is soft — it stops new rounds, not the one running nor the closing call — so with a $0.40 cap and the mind route's 32,000 tokens a run could land at $0.6–0.8; $0.30 plus one capped round and the closing call stays under about $0.50. `reach_service` refuses `tell_owner` from it (`RULES["free"]`); `screen_service.in_surplus_turn` counts `mind:free`, so a line is never spoken. It writes the usual `mind` ledger row (`source="free"`), is attention-blind (it does not move "your last wake"), is never retried, and stops at the daily cap (`mind:free`). (Before 2026-10-11 free time was a surplus-queue job with far more room; that is retired with the queue — [surplus.md](surplus.md).) Test: `tests/test_free_time.py`.
- **Pings in the pack**: the recent pings and how each ended (`pings.hooks.pack_lines`, 6 h) right after "Recent messages", under "Pings — answered by your reply path, not here"; a message line that is a ping says ` (ping — answered)` (`ping_status` on the reader's dict); `speaker()` trusts the reader's structural `by_edward` before the signature guess; an escalation carries the whole message (`event_text`: `media.present.event_text` — `raw_data.full_text` with each bare `attachment:<rowid>` placeholder filled from the describer's `raw_data.attachment_descriptions`, else from `media.hooks.describe_ref`'s cache — before 2026-10-11 every photo reached the pack and the noticer as a bare placeholder; up to 1,500 chars in the pack; the noticer's `noticing_service.event_text` reads the same presenter; `tests/test_media_event_text.py`). **Rule 6 of the prompt** follows `EDWARD_PINGS` (`mind_system_prompt()`, read when the wake runs): with pings on it is "Pings are not yours here. An `@Edward` (the owner's own included), a reply to your message, or the owner's self-chat is answered by your reply path, never in a wake — unless this wake was woken for that mention (`[WAKE: mention]`, rule 3: then reply); the pack lists them. Since the owner reads their own texts within minutes, never tell them what a message they have read says." With pings off (`RULE_6_PINGS_OFF`) it is "Is the owner already there?" and the mention wake answers. The exemption keeps rules 3 and 6 from contradicting each other while `EDWARD_PINGS=off`, when the mention wake is the only path that answers an `@Edward` — the conflict the judgement check's `mention_answerable` scenario is there to catch. The act line names `tell_owner` (a note, or `urgent=true` for a push; `leave_note` / `send_push_notification` where it is not bound); "This Mac's screen (if one is set up) is yours" stays. Test: `tests/test_mind_pings_hooks.py`.
- **The owner steers from iMessage**: their own `@Edward …` text in any chat ("stop pushing me about soccer") is the owner speaking. The ping runner calls `memory_service.remember_owners_words(…)` after a ping turn whose ping is theirs; it extracts **only their text** — never the thread, never a friend's line — under the `chat` profile with `turn_source="owner_ping"`, one of `memory_service.OWNER_SPEAKS_SOURCES` (and `llm_spend.OWNER_TURN_SOURCES`, pinned equal, and decay's `memory_decay_service.OWNERS_TURN_SOURCES`), so the row is heard/owner, `is_owners_instruction` renders it under their standing instructions, and decay never lapses it as someone else's. It never fails the ping. Test: `tests/test_owners_words.py`.
- **Routing**: `route_for_wake(reason, turn_source, settings_model)` — on Claude every wake (follow-through, the speaker, the nightly, free time) runs on the `mind` route and the pulse on the `pulse` route ([llm-and-quota.md](llm-and-quota.md#claude-the-default-provider)); a `speak` wake runs on `EDWARD_SPEAK_MODEL` when it is set (as named on Claude; through the catalog on Codex). Purposes are `mind:<reason>` (autonomous: they stop at the daily cap) and `pulse`.
- **`/api/mind/now` never shows a noticing's claim**: a `speak` row's line is the fixed "Something came up — I left a note." (`wake_state.SPEAK_LINE`), because the presence sheet, Today's hero and the stage read these rows; `wake_state.reason_label` names the reasons ("Something I noticed", "Nightly reflection", also in the journal's `REASON_LABELS`). Test: `tests/test_wake_state_labels.py`.
- **The judgement check** ([testing.md](testing.md)): the mind's four situations (`mention_answerable` — a mention he can answer; `standing_instruction` — a mention in a chat the owner said to stay out of, the instruction rendered in the pack's real format, "  - Please stay out of … (said Sep 10)"; `follow_through_reply` — a promise kept in the ping's chat; `nightly_silent`), the real model with every outward act recorded at the transport and never sent, process guards installed before any tool runs (`scripts/judgement_eval.py::install_eval_guards` → `services.eval_guards.install_for_eval`, which takes only `EDWARD_LLM_PROVIDER` and `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` from `.env` when the shell has not set them, prints the provider, then installs the in-repo guards), no forced tool, `--json-out`. Tests: `tests/test_judgement_eval.py`, `tests/test_mind_policy.py`.
- **Running older code against this database**: an Edward from before the rebuild would run the `*/5` `proactive_sweep` as an ordinary LLM event turn (~288 a day) and the `nightly`/`free` ticks as full-tool `manual` wakes. Turn the flags down first (`EDWARD_PROACTIVITY=off`, `EDWARD_CARE_WATCH=off`, `EDWARD_PUSH_BUDGET=<n>`), then cancel those seeds with the SQL in [scheduler.md](scheduler.md).

## The wake (since 2026-09-18, and what still holds)

(`backend/services/mind_service.py`, `routers/mind.py`. Bullets that describe what 2026-10-11 retired say so.)
- **One primitive replaces every judgment call**: `mind_service.wake(reason)` runs an ordinary `chat_with_memory` turn in its thread (the pinned `edward-mind` for `manual` and `self`; source `edward_thought`, shown beside `edward-surfaces` in the sidebar) with `extraction_profile="mind"`, the thread's history window, timeout and lock. Until 2026-10-11 it ran with a wake tool list of about 40 names for the reasons `manual`, `morning`, `evening`, `self`, `messages`, `mention`, `reflect`, `surfaces` and the surplus queue's `reflect_lite`, `research`, `prep`, `free`; the reasons and the policy now are in the section above.
- **The pack is raw** (`collect_pack` does the I/O, each source isolated; `render_pack` is pure), in this order: time and presence, "Since your last wake: N new messages", what was not readable this wake, a self wake's Follow-through block and the extra ask; the state document; standing instructions and live stand-downs; the last 30 messages verbatim; the pings; "Waiting on you" (a mention's only) and held approvals; today's calendar, the pet board, what each surface shows ("This Mac's screen now: …"); his notes for Today; every push in 12 h with its outcome; his own intentions and thoughts (`memories about=self origin=thought`, last 48 h plus every live intention, with ids); his last few wakes; his last ten `did` memories; the owner's verdicts since the last wake (heartbeat feedback + UI signals); then the reason's own asks (the nightly's day or week and digest). No scores, boosts, priorities or suppression anywhere in it.
- **What it did is a ledger row**: `ledger_events` kind `mind`, source = the reason, outcome `acted` / `left_alone` / `failed` / `skipped` (quota exhausted), reason = his closing line, detail = tools, surfaces, delivered. `GET /api/mind/recent`. `POST /api/mind/wake {reason, extra}` answers **202** at once (`{accepted, reason, joined}`) and runs the wake as a tracked task (`request_wake(reason, delay_s=0)`): a wake runs for minutes and a tunnel in front of Edward may give up after 100 s; a press while the same wake is pending or running joins it. `last_wake_at()` reads the ledger so a restart does not lose it.
- **What makes a wake matter**: remembering, a note (`tell_owner`; on Today, never a buzz without `urgent`), and a wake for later (`schedule(action="add", kind="wake")`; the legacy `wake_me(when, why)` — a `scheduled_events` row with `event_metadata={"type":"mind_wake","why":…}` in `edward-mind` that the scheduler routes to `wake("self", extra=why)`). `request_wake(reason, delay_s=60)` coalesces repeated asks into one wake (pacing, not judgment).
- **The ticks are wakes** (*the 07:00 morning and 18:00 evening ticks and the Sunday 09:00 reflect were retired on 2026-10-11 — the 03:00 nightly and the 13:00 free run are the ticks now*): the scheduler seeds them (`scheduler_service.WAKEUP_TICKS`), routed to `mind_service.wake(reason)`. The pre-mind tick kinds (`morning_briefing`, `evening_briefing`, `stale_thread_check`, `push_outcome_check`) are cancelled on boot (`is_retired_wakeup_seed`). `wakeup_service.py`, `wakeup_signals.py` (3,700 lines of scored packs) and `routers/wakeup.py` are gone; the Journal (`/api/journal/week` → `wakes`) and the health report (`wakes`) read the mind's ledger.
- **Events fire with continuity**: a fired event runs in the thread that made it when that thread still exists and is the owner's or Edward's own (`scheduler_service.event_thread`), else in `edward-mind`; `build_event_trigger` carries `fire_count`, `last_fired_at`, `last_result` and the intention behind the event; a scheduled `why` is remembered as an `intention` memory (`intention_valid_until`: a day past a one-shot fire, 30 days for recurring) linked through `event_metadata.memory_id`, and cancelling the event retires it ([scheduler.md](scheduler.md)).
- **Escalations reach the mind only as a mention** (*since 2026-10-11*): the pack's "Waiting on you" section lists them raw (id, sender, chat, text, time, and a `sensitive` flag from `push_safety_gate.classify_high_risk_push_label_text`), and after the wake they are stamped `notified_at` / `notification_batch_id="mind:<ledger id>"` so the next wake sees new ones only. `notification_dispatcher.py` (1,896 lines of ranking, suppression and a JSON push/digest/defer call) is gone; `group_events_by_sender` moved to `contact_identity_service`. Every autonomous reach goes through `reach_service` ([push-and-notes.md](push-and-notes.md)).
- **The heartbeat thinks in the mind — the only path** (`services/heartbeat/mind_path.run_mind_cycle`; [heartbeat.md](heartbeat.md)): `triage_service.run_triage_cycle` always runs the mind path; `heartbeat_config.mind_enabled` no longer branches (it defaults on, and `GET /api/heartbeat/status` always reports true). The legacy three-layer triage engine, its sub-engines, their learners and their tests are deleted — the step the original install called "Phase 6b". `identity_filter` drops what the owner wrote unless it is an `owner_direct` ping or their own `@edward`, drops inbound in a chat the owner replied to in the batch, and keeps one `@edward` mention per chat — **only an iMessage is a mention** (`MENTION_SOURCES`, 2026-10-11: anyone can put `@edward` in a subject line, and it bought an immediate, paid wake that had to reply for an event with no chat); it marks the rest `escalated` and hands them to the noticer through the `messages` shim, or, for a mention, asks for an immediate `mention` wake carrying its `mention_ids`. Each escalation in a mention's pack carries the last eight lines of its thread (`_thread_lines`).
- **The proactive jobs are gone** (*and the morning wake, its meeting-prep hint and the Sunday reflect went on 2026-10-11 — Sunday's nightly reads the week now*): `services/proactive/morning_briefing.py`, `meeting_prep.py`, `open_loop_nudge.py`, `pattern_detection.py` and `relationship_maintenance_job.py` are deleted, and `main.py` starts no proactive services; the `*/5` `proactive_sweep` event runs the late-dose alert and the noticing upkeep. `/api/proactive/status` lists the wakes (`wakes: [{kind, cron}]`) and keeps only the dossier thresholds as config; `ProactivePanel` shows `WakesNote` (pure `proactiveWakes.ts`). `daily_execution_planner.py` and `meeting_context_engine.py` were deleted with the engine layer.
- **A self he can hold in his head**: one document titled exactly `Edward: state` (tags `state,self`; `mind_service.STATE_DOC_TITLE`, `document_service.get_document_by_title`) is the summary he carries — every wake's pack opens with it under "Who you are and what you are doing", clipped on a word boundary at 4,000 chars (`clip_state_document`, with a note to shorten it); with none, the pack invites him to write one. Sunday's nightly asks him to rewrite it ("a page, not a log"); the manual says to edit it whenever something about himself changes. The pack lists `Open intentions (N):` and `Recent thoughts (M):` separately — the count and the instruction to close stale ones are the bound, not a cap. **Thoughts decay**: `default_temporal_nature(memory_type, origin=)` returns `temporary` for `origin="thought"` unless the type is `intention` (`evolving`). `memory_decay_service.is_protected(memory, protected_ids)` holds an `intention` a pending/processing scheduled event links to (`pending_intention_ids()` reads `event_metadata.memory_id`), and the decay pass runs after every Sunday nightly, its counts and `stamp` in the ledger row's `detail.decay` (`mind_service.wake_ledger_detail` keeps a wake row parseable under the 600-char cap by shortening `tools` first, with `tools_omitted` — a long reflection used to lose the stamp `run_memory_decay.py --revert-stamp` needs).
- **The pulse is a surfaces wake**: `wake()` takes a profile — `message` (then no pack is collected), `conversation_id`, `tools`, `system_prompt`, `turn_source`, `skip_extraction`, `max_history_chars`, `record` — and `expression_service.run_expression_pulse` calls `mind_service.wake("surfaces", message=build_pulse_trigger(...), conversation_id="edward-surfaces", tools=PULSE_TOOLS, system_prompt=EXPRESSION_SYSTEM_PROMPT, turn_source="pulse", skip_extraction=True, record=False)`. The pulse keeps `collect_signals` / `should_pulse` / its cadence and its own `decision`/`pulse` ledger rows, the mind keeps its `last_wake_at`, and a failed surfaces wake returns `{"ok": False, "error_class"}` so the pulse records the failure and refreshes the fallback card ([screen-and-browser.md](screen-and-browser.md)). `tests/test_pulse_is_a_wake.py`; the pulse tests patch `mind_service.chat_with_memory`.
- **The owner's window**: Edward tab → Mind → **Thoughts** (`MindPanel.tsx`, pure `lib/mindLog.ts`; slug `thoughts` in `ROOM_SLUGS.mind`). `GET /api/mind/log?days=7` is every wake with the pushes it sent (from `push_log` by the mind's thread: a push belongs to the first wake row after it, `LOG_PUSH_WINDOW_S`) and the owner's verdict; `GET /api/mind/intentions` is every live `intention` memory with the pending event waiting on it (`next_fire_at`, `next_fire_why`); `POST /api/mind/verdict {ledger_id, verdict: right_call|wrong_call, note?}` writes a `user_signals` row of kind `mind_verdict` (`ref_id` = the ledger id). The next wake's pack renders each verdict under the owner's verdicts with `on your wake: <source>: <outcome> — <note>` (`_attach_wakes_to_verdicts`), so the loop closes through the pack. The panel folds runs of left-alone wakes into one quiet row and has a "Wake now" button. The Journal panel beside it stays as the week's observations view. `tests/test_mind_window.py`; frontend `__tests__/mindLog.test.ts`.
- **The engine layer is gone** (2026-09-20): `_collect_llm_context` is a handful of reads (entity memories, enrichments, documents, heartbeat briefing, session briefing, open loops, tools); `## Recent Awareness` is the briefing plus ambient notes only; the post-turn learners are extraction, search tags and reflection; no send-time ledgers in the tool executor, no fact-card prefix in `routers/chat.py`, no experiment block in `graph/nodes.py`, no preference/fact-graph side effects in extraction; the listeners store events and nothing else; `/api/memories/context` reads `memories about=contact`; `push_engagement` keeps only `mark_event_acted_on` / `mark_recent_pushes_acted`; the default-tool prompt floor is pinned under 20,000 chars (`test_engine_unplug.py`). 58 engine modules (~47,500 lines) and their tests were deleted, 80 model classes and their raw migrations removed from `database.py`, and `init_db` drops the old tables in one dated `DROP TABLE IF EXISTS … CASCADE` batch (`test_engine_tables_dropped.py`; `conftest.clean_db`'s list is pinned against the models). The old heartbeat engine's batch-A tables stay in the schema though nothing writes them ([data-model.md](data-model.md)). **The memory graph (`graph_ingest_queue`, the Graphiti worker) is separate and untouched.**
- **Reliability** (2026-09-24): the pack is gathered *before* the thread's lock, every source at once on `PACK_SOURCE_TIMEOUT_SECONDS` (15), the whole on `PACK_TIMEOUT_SECONDS` (45); a failed source is listed under "Not readable this wake" (an unreadable calendar is never an empty day) and an ungatherable pack is a `failed` row (`PackTimeout`); the pulse's `collect_signals` has the same bounds. **One lock per thread** (`thread_lock`): the pulse does not hold the mind's, so a mention never waits behind it. Retrieval embeds `memory_query_for(pack)` (≤ 400 chars), not the whole pack. A failed wake counts `heartbeat_events.mind_attempts` on each escalation it was served; the third gives up (`notified_at`, batch `mind:gave-up`, still listed on Today). A `mention` request during a running wake queues one more if anything is still waiting. The quota check reads `gate.meter` — a property; an old `gate.meter()` call raised, was swallowed, and made the exhausted/paced checks always "ok" — and warns hourly if the check itself fails. Tests: `tests/test_mind_wake_reliability.py`, `test_quota_state.py`.
- **Failed wakes retry — safely** (2026-09-25/26; a one-hour server-side Codex outage lost a wake outright): a `self`, `mention` or `nightly` wake that failed **transiently** (`is_transient_error`: a retryable provider error, a 5xx, a 401 still rejected after the refresh — unless a freshly refreshed token was refused too, which fails the same way every time — a transport or connection error, an `LLMCallTimeout`; never a rate limit or a cap, which the gate owns, a bad request, a bug, or the wake's own deadline, since that turn may already have acted) is asked for again after 10, 20 and 40 min (`retry_delay`, `MAX_WAKE_RETRIES` 3) in its own slot (`retry:<reason>`, so a retry and an ordinary ask never swallow each other), keeping its profile, its purpose label and a "(Retry n of 3: … Whatever it did before failing stands; check before repeating it.)" line. A wake whose model never answered and that did nothing retries too. **Never after it reached someone or committed to something** (`thought_journal.reached_someone` / `should_journal` on the failed turn's actions): a send cannot be taken back, and a retry told to reply could text the same group again, reworded; a wake that only wrote memory still retries. A mention wake carries its `mention_ids` (from `mind_path`; coalesced and rearmed asks merge them): a retry or follow-up whose mentions nobody waits on any more hands what else waits to the inbound shim, or does nothing (`mention_still_served`, read from the table, not the pack). Tests: `tests/test_mind_wake_reliability.py`, `test_wake_retry_safety.py`.
- **Continuity** (2026-09-25): the thread window cannot hold even the last pack, so every pack lists "Your last few wakes (newest first)" — the last five ledger rows, reason, outcome, when, and the closing line cut to 200 chars (`recent_wake_lines`), just before "What you did lately". A `speak` or `free` wake (and the retired `prep`, `research` and `reflect_lite`, whose rows are still read) serves no escalation and answers no verdict, so it does not move `last_wake_at` (`ATTENTION_BLIND_REASONS`, `moves_last_wake`): the owner's verdicts, decided approvals and "since your last wake" stay with the wakes that can act on them. Test: `tests/test_mind_continuity.py`.
- **What he is doing right now** (`services/wake_state.py`, `GET /api/mind/now`, 2026-09-25; the design rule "the animation is a claim"): `wake()` calls `wake_state.begin/end` around every wake, and the pulse's turn is a wake too (a mind wake wins when both run). `snapshot()` is the body — `waking` (reason, started_at, conversation), `last` / `recent` (five ledger rows with the journal entry id), `next_wake_at` (the earliest pending wake or tick row, or a requested wake's due time), `approvals_waiting` (unexpired held sends), `unread_thoughts` (the sidebar's unread dots), `quota` (used %, exhausted, resets_at — the day's spend cap on Claude, [llm-and-quota.md](llm-and-quota.md)) and `hands` (the Chrome extension link, `down_for_s`; while Chrome hands are off — `EDWARD_CHROME_HANDS` unset — the status carries no `connected`, so hands read unknown, never down). ## Standing instructions, and the mind never texts the owner

- **Standing instructions open every pack** (2026-09-25): the owner's live `instruction` memories and every active `chat_standdowns` row, above the messages, under a heading that says they outrank everything below, a mention included (`instruction_lines`, `_instructions_source`). **Only `origin=heard about=owner` rows** — what the owner said; an instruction Edward inferred from someone else's message (`read`) or decided himself (`did`/`thought`) still reaches a wake through retrieval with its tag, never under the owner's name. On the original install a pre-deploy review found 44 of 95 live instruction rows were not the owner's, among them tool-test prompts stored as instructions.
- **Which rows count as the owner's** (2026-09-27; `_instructions_source`, `is_owners_instruction`): wakes had been minting the owner's standing instructions — a wake's pack is the turn's human message, the mind's extraction kept "anything the owner said in the pack" as `heard`, a missing origin defaulted to `heard`, and every later wake re-extracted and reinforced the row. The pack now leaves out rows whose provenance names profile `mind`, `pulse` or `scheduled_event` or a turn source that is not the owner speaking, rows from Edward's own threads (`EDWARD_OWN_THREAD_IDS`), and rows from conversations with source `edward_thought`, `scheduled_event`, `heartbeat` or `orchestrator_worker` (`NOT_OWNERS_CONVERSATION_SOURCES`) — except a journal entry the owner replied in (`OWNER_SPEAKS_THREAD_SOURCES`: when the ref names the entry's own thread and says the owner was speaking, it is theirs). Provenance decides only when it names the row's own thread; otherwise the thread decides (a later wake's reinforcement once stamped its own ref on rows from the owner's chat). Past 20 rows the section says how many older ones it left out.
- **A row whose thread is missing is the owner's only when its own provenance vouches**: a row with no provenance whose conversation had been deleted used to stay theirs, which is how a blanket "in-app only" rule nobody remembered giving outranked every mention. Now, when the thread is NULL or not a conversation that exists, the row is the owner's only if its ref is an `owner_confirmed` pin or says the owner was speaking (`_owner_spoke_on_record`): `turn_source` chat, voice, widget or alexa, or a turn's own write (`extraction`, `remember`) with no turn recorded under the chat profile (until 2026-09-27 the PWA's chat stream lost its turn record after the first step — [tool-loop.md](tool-loop.md) — so rows written then carry refs with the thread and no turn, and the write took that as the owner; the pack does too). A chat instruction stays theirs after they delete that chat; a ref naming another conversation vouches for nothing. Anything else there (no ref, `unrecorded`, an audit merge row) is left out. `_instructions_source` looks up the ref's conversation for a row with no thread (`conversations_named`), so such a row is not mistaken for one whose thread is gone. **To keep a rule for good, pin it**: `source_ref {"kind": "owner_confirmed"}` ([memory.md](memory.md)).
- A mention wake that ends without answering in the chat it was asked in is told so once by the tool loop's reply check — a send there or a stand-down for it answers it (a `tell_owner` answers only the owner's own turn: a note to the owner left the friend who asked with nothing), and nothing is ever forced ([tool-loop.md](tool-loop.md#the-tool-loop-graphtool_looppy)), so a stay-out instruction can be honoured.
- **The mind never texts the owner** (the judgement check, 2026-09-25: a late-dose scenario texted the owner with `send_message`): a `mind`, `pulse` or `hub_voice` turn's legacy send tool addressed to the owner — their configured names, "me", "myself", "owner", "the owner", or any of their handles (`heartbeat_config.imessage_owner_handles` plus `EDWARD_OWNER_HANDLES`) in any spelling (`tools.names_owner`, `same_handle`) — is refused in the tool loop's boundary check, before the taint gate, and again in `_send_guard`: "Tool error: <the owner> is reached by send_push_notification (if it can't wait) or leave_note — not by text." (`graph/tools.TEXT_TO_OWNER_REFUSAL`; its "If you meant someone else named …" sentence appears only when `EDWARD_OWNER_NAME` is set). Under the toolset the one send, `send_message`, keys the refusal on the turn's `TurnPolicy` instead of its source: any turn the owner is not part of (a wake, the pulse, a friend's ping, a worker, an event they did not make) is refused and pointed at `tell_owner`, except a reply into the chat the turn was asked in or a self-chat where their own ping waits (`graph/tools._chats_where_owner_is_waiting`; [tool-loop.md](tool-loop.md#the-one-send-toolsetmessagingpy)). Chat, voice, scheduled-event, heartbeat and worker turns the owner made are untouched ("text me at 5" works). A turn whose policy may not reach anyone can't `schedule` a task, reminder or (unless allowed) a wake (`TurnPolicy.may_schedule` / `may_wake_later`). The judgement check reports refused attempts under each scenario (`Observed.refused`) without scoring them. Test: `tests/test_mind_never_texts_owner.py`.
- **The prompt**: a mention must be answered unless a standing instruction says stay out; don't start or join other people's conversations unasked (it goes out from the owner's number) — tell the owner instead; push only for what can't wait until they next look at Today (at night, only what can't wait until morning); when unsure, leave a note saying what you would do. Test: `tests/test_standing_instructions.py`.
- **The judgement check** (`services/judgement_eval.py`, `scripts/judgement_eval.py`): until 2026-10-11 seven situations on Codex with every send held by the taint gate; now the mind's four on Claude with every outward act recorded at the transport and, under `EDWARD_DRY_RUN_OUTWARD=1`, answered by the executor before the tool runs (Chrome, the shell, the screens and every send included); exit 2 on a safety failure. Run it before turning the mind on unattended and whenever the mind prompt, its tools or the `mind` route change. The :5433 test cluster is not a LaunchAgent and does not come back after a reboot, so the check starts it first (`sandbox_env.ensure_test_cluster` → `scripts/test-pg-cluster.sh ensure`, plus `createdb` for another `*_test` name) and exits 1 saying why when it cannot (`tests/test_judgement_eval_starts_the_test_cluster.py`). How to run it: [testing.md](testing.md). Test: `tests/test_judgement_eval.py`.

## The thought journal — reading his thoughts into action

(`services/thought_journal.py`, 2026-09-25.) The design principle: Edward needs no approval to act, but it must be obvious when he chooses to. (The mind rebuild had deleted every caller of `create_thought_conversation`, so every wake landed in one long `edward-mind` thread of raw packs.)
- A wake that does something **outward** (a send, a push, a note, a hosted page, typing in a browser) or **commits** to something (scheduling or cancelling, a stand-down) writes one `edward_thought` conversation, titled from its first action ("Replied to Parker in Family"; a 1:1 chat is "Replied to Parker", never "… in Parker" — `_is_one_to_one`), whose narrative is **Why I looked** (from the pack) / **What I noticed** (the reasoning summaries) / **What I did** (one line per action from the executor's record — `TurnRecord.actions`, noted by the loop's `_journal_note`; a send counts as sent only when the tool's own reply says so; internal work folds into "Also:"; a screen change reads "Mac screen" or "household screen", a shell command "Ran on the Mac: …") / **Why** (his closing sentences — the prompt asks for two or three when he acts, one line when he leaves it). The pulse never journals.
- The entry id goes on the ledger row (`detail.thought_id`), the wake result and `/api/mind/log`; the unread dot is set only when a message reached someone or a push reached the owner. Conversations carry `thought {kind: mind_action, ledger_id, reason, acted_at, outward, verdict, reached}` — `reached` (`thought_journal.reached_someone`: a message delivered or a push that reached the owner; null on an entry written before it) is the only thing the "reached out" chip reads, never `outward`, which a note or a held send also sets; Today's `did` has `kind: action` rows linking to the entry.
- Frontend: the sidebar's Edward group leads with **Actions** (never folded, bolt icon, "reached out" chip; the rest is "Older thoughts"); an entry opens with "Edward acted · <reason> · <when>" and **Right call? Yes / No** (`ThoughtEntryHeader`, posts `/api/mind/verdict`, which the next wake's pack reads); the Mind panel links each acting wake "Read his thinking →"; raw packs in any thread fold to one line ("Wake pack · nightly · thu 3:00am · 14.2K chars", `PackCard`). Tests: `tests/test_thought_journal.py`, `test_thought_journal_capture.py`, `test_thought_journal_wake.py`; frontend `__tests__/packMessage.test.ts`, `packCard.test.tsx`, `thoughtEntry.test.ts`, `thoughtEntryHeader.test.tsx`, `sidebarGroups.test.ts`.
- **Entries stay readable and reachable**: replying inside an entry seeds the entry as the thread's first assistant message (checkpointed with the reply) and every turn there gets the entry's context in the system prompt (reason, time, the wake's thread and turn, the recorded actions, the start of the pack; ≤ 8K — `thought_journal.entry_turn_context`, `streaming._entry_turn_context`), so "why?" is answered from what he actually saw and did; `GET /api/conversations/{id}` puts the entry first whenever the thread doesn't hold it. Journal entries are always visible (`conversation_service.is_journal_entry` / `_journal_entry_filter`), and Edward's group pages entries apart from other thoughts. `mind_service.wake` calls `thought_journal.begin_entry()` before the turn (`clear_entry()` for the pulse), so a push or note from a wake links to `/chat?c=<entry>` (`entry_url`) and opening the entry acks the push; `finish_entry` writes a minimal entry if the journal could not, so a link never 404s. `prune_cobwebs.py` never deletes an entry. Tests: `tests/test_thought_entry_reply.py`, `test_thought_entry_visibility.py`, `test_thought_entry_links.py`, `test_thought_entry_wake_links.py`.
- **Unattended code is an entry too** (2026-09-27): a turn that is not a wake, not the pulse and not one the owner is part of (a fired scheduled event, a heartbeat reply, a text to Edward's Twilio number, a worker), whose record shows a Codex session or an MCP server that really ran (not held, not refused), is handed by `turn_ledger.end_turn` to `thought_journal.schedule_unattended_entry`, which writes a `ledger_events` row of kind **`acted`** (source = the turn's source) and a journal entry linked both ways ("Why I looked — A scheduled event: …"). Whether a server started is read from the whole reply (`thought_journal.action_facts`). `recent_action_entries` reads `acted` rows too, so they show under Today's "What I did", and verdicts accept them; the wake counts, `last_wake_at` and the mind log still read only kind `mind`. (The tools that started Codex sessions and installed MCP servers left the toolset on 2026-10-11; the `acted` path stays for whatever an unattended turn runs next.)
- **What the journal says about memory work**: closing an intention is internal — read as a success when the reply starts `ok`, shown in the entry's "Also:" line as "Closed an intention: <outcome>"; a turn that only closes an intention writes no entry. An update that only moves a horizon reads "Retired a memory (m-1)" / "Set a memory to lapse Thu 9:00am (m-2)", never an empty "Updated a memory:".

## What a wake may do to memory

- **A wake's pack is not the owner speaking** (2026-09-27; `memory_service.coerce_extracted_fields`): under the `mind` profile an extracted `heard`, or a missing origin, becomes `read` (or `thought` when it is about Edward himself), a `user_stated` source becomes `inferred` (so decay's stated-and-timeless protection cannot make a wake's conclusion immortal), and `MIND_EXTRACTION_ADDENDUM` says a wake writes no instruction for the owner and names the pack sections not to re-extract. A near-duplicate (≥ 0.92) from a `mind`, `pulse` or `scheduled_event` turn only refreshes `last_used_at`, and an UPDATE/MERGE from those turns adds no reinforcement — a wake re-reading its own pack is not independent evidence. Remembering in a pulse or scheduled-event turn follows the same rules. Details: [memory.md](memory.md). Tests: `tests/test_mind_extraction_provenance.py`, `test_standing_instructions.py`.
- **Closing an intention** (2026-09-27; `graph/tools.close_intention`, the `memory` tool's `close`): `close_intention(memory_id, outcome)` sets `valid_until=now`, appends the outcome to the content and to `source_ref.closed`, keeps the row and its original embedding (`update_memory(reembed=False)` — the closed text scores 0.70–0.82 against the original, and a re-embedded row could be matched and reopened), and says if a scheduled event is still linked. It refuses a non-intention, an already closed or lapsed one, an unknown id and an empty outcome with `Tool error: …`; success starts `ok (…)`. Before it the only ways out were deleting the row or rewording it — a free-time wake once noted "marking it closed in the text didn't retire it" after research ran four times on one topic, and Edward put exactly this tool on his own wishlist. The nightly's asks say to close with it. An update also takes an optional `valid_until` (a zone-less time is the owner's local time; now or earlier retires the row). Conflict detection matches live rows only, so a closed intention can be decided again and a retired row is never reopened by a passive re-lift. Tests: `tests/test_close_intention.py`, `test_retired_rows_stay_retired.py`.
- **A wake cannot change what the owner said** (2026-09-27): the update and forget tools were wake tools, and from a wake they rewrote, retired and hard-deleted rules the owner had just pinned. From any turn that is not the owner's own (a wake, the pulse, a fired event, a heartbeat reply, a worker) they refuse a `heard`/`owner` row, and always an `owner_confirmed` one, with `Tool error: <the owner> told me that themselves, so only they can change it…`; if one looks wrong, he asks the owner. Intentions, his own rows and what he read stay his to change. Conflict detection follows the same line: a wake's words (or his own thought in the owner's chat) are kept beside the owner's row, never written into it, and a pin is never rewritten at all. Details: [memory.md](memory.md). Test: `tests/test_memory_tools_keep_owners_rows.py`.
