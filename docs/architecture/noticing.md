# Noticing, then speaking

How Edward notices what matters in the owner's messages and decides whether it reaches them: `EDWARD_PROACTIVITY`, the noticer pass, the pure gate, the speaker, the sweep, outcomes and the learning ladder, the eval. Built 2026-10-11. How a reach then lands on the owner's phone or Today is [push-and-notes.md](push-and-notes.md); the wakes themselves are [mind.md](mind.md).

**On a fresh install noticing is off** (`EDWARD_PROACTIVITY=off`): inbound is stored and stamped, pings are still answered by their own path ([pings.md](pings.md)), promises are still kept, and nothing else speaks up. Noticing reads iMessages, so it also needs the iMessage listener ([heartbeat.md](heartbeat.md)). Turn it up in two steps — `shadow`, then `notice` — after running the eval below on your own setup.

## The path

(`backend/services/noticing_service.py`, `services/speak_gate.py`, `services/imessage_read_state.py`, `services/reach_service.py`)
- **Why**: the old `messages` wake (a full model pack for every inbound batch, about $6.8 a day on the original install) relayed texts the owner had already read and spoke where they were mid-conversation; none of 33 pushes in the evidence window was opened. The catches the owner valued were *noticings* — two facts that disagree (an airline's "check-in closes at 10" against the owner's own "en route" in another chat), a deadline closing, someone waiting only on them, a credential pasted into a chat. So noticing is cheap and private, and speaking is rare, gated in code, and verified.
- **`EDWARD_PROACTIVITY`** (read at call time): `off` (the default) stores and stamps — `request_notice` stamps each non-ping event `stored:<pass>` so Today never floods, with no model call; `shadow` runs the noticer and the gate and records each decision (`status='shadow'`) without speaking; `notice` runs the whole path. An unknown value is `off`. **Never stamped, in any mode**: a ping (`triage_status='ping'` or `raw_data.ping`), an iMessage mention (`@edward`, `has_mentions`; an email or calendar row with `@edward` in it is noticed like any other) or the owner's self-chat line (`owner_direct`) — the ping runner or the mention wake owns those, and the mention wake reads "still waiting" from `notified_at`.
- **The pass** (`request_notice(event_ids=None, *, source)` → a 60 s coalescing timer → `notice_pass`; `event_ids=None` is the `request_wake("messages")` shim — the escalated, unstamped events of the last day plus the owner's own unstamped lines of the last 6 h, which the identity filter dismisses but which carry their commitments and the keys they paste; a pass reads an event once):
  - a code pre-filter (pings; echoes by `raw_data.edward_echo`, Edward's signature, or a from-me line within 90 s of an `imessage_outgoing_sends` row for that chat; tapbacks and empty lines; stood-down chats — each stamped `notice-skip:<why>:<pass>`);
  - a **secret in the owner's own line** becomes a code `security` noticing (the value never stored; the model sees the line redacted);
  - the quota (`llm_spend.quota_state_cached()` under Claude, the Codex meter otherwise; `exhausted` → `notice-deferred:`; a broken check is `exhausted`, never "ok");
  - the context (`build_context`, ≤ 32K chars): now and presence; the new lines tagged `hb:<event id>` with read state ("read after 3 min", `imessage_read_state`); the last 10 lines of each chat; **the owner's own lines from every chat over 6 h** (dropped oldest-first to fit); the open noticings with their full claims and keys; intentions, scheduled events and the calendar for 48 h under `COMING_UP_HEADER` — "background: never a source, never a noticing on its own"; stood-down chats;
  - one call, `build_background_llm(route="background", priority="interactive", model=EDWARD_NOTICE_MODEL)` — on Claude the background route, Haiku 5.5 at high ([llm-and-quota.md](llm-and-quota.md#where-the-routes-came-from)) — under **`purpose_scope("mind:notice")`** (autonomous: it stops at the daily cap, never in the owner's reserve), 45 s, the static `NOTICER_PROMPT`, which names every `category` word with a one-line meaning ("a check-in or boarding deadline is travel, never logistics") and the five `suggested` words. **Whether a check-in can push depends on the word `travel`** (only travel and household are floor items), and until a review on 2026-10-11 the prompt listed none of them while saying "never … logistics"; `validate_items` maps obvious synonyms (`CATEGORY_SYNONYMS`: flight, airline, check-in → travel; delivery, pets → household; …; `SUGGESTION_SYNONYMS`: notify, alert → push) before an unknown word falls to None — never an ambiguous one ("service", "air") to a floor category, and never a guess of `thread`, which the gate turns into a push outside an open ping chat (`tests/test_noticer_categories.py`).
- **What the noticer returns**: `{"noticings": [...]}`; an item is dropped (counted `invalid`) for an unknown kind, a source not in the pack, a one-source conflict or an unreadable `closes_at` — **each drop names its rule** (`drop_reason`: `not_an_object`, `unknown_kind`, `no_sources`, `source_not_in_pack`, `conflict_needs_two`, `unreadable_closes_at`) with only the shape of what was wrong (a kind word, an id, a date's format as `9999-99-99`, else a length), never the claim or a message line: one log line per drop, and the counts in the pass's ledger row (`invalid_rules`).
  - **Sources are only the `hb:` ids of message lines**; "Coming up" (intentions, scheduled events, the calendar) is background, never a source and never a noticing on its own — the prompt and the pack's header both say so. Once drops were named (2026-10-11), every one of them in 67 real Haiku passes turned out to be a noticing about a calendar line (a bill due at noon) cited as `calendar:<timestamp>` or not at all, and a one-item answer that was that note is what made the "family member unanswered for 4 h" case miss about 40 % of runs; after the rule, none in 67.
  - **A noticing's chat is its sources'** (the first inbound source's, else the first's): the pack names a chat but never shows its identifier, so the model's `chat_identifier` was a guess ("Mom", "the soccer chat") that the gate's chat lookups — the owner is on it, a live chat, a stand-down, an open ping — never found; the prompt no longer asks for it, and a `who` written as one string is one name (`tests/test_noticer_drops.py`).
  - A repeat of an open, held or shadow noticing (same key, or same chat and kind with overlapping sources, 72 h) merges its new sources instead of a second row.
  - **A pass that does not return the object is `failed`** (`notice-failed:<pass>`, ledger `error_class="parse"`), never an empty day; the sweep retries it once after 10 min, and a second failure stamps `notice-failed2:`.
  - Log lines: `[notice] request events=N`, `[notice] pass <id> dropped an item: rule=<rule> …`, `[notice] pass <id> ok events=N noticed=K invalid=I (<rule>:<n>,…) ms=…`, `… FAILED (<class>)`, `… deferred (cap)`.
- **The gate** (`speak_gate.decide`, pure; first match wins):
  - R0 the cap (`journal`; a floor item → a templated `fallback` push), R0b paced (only floor items), R1 evidence;
  - R2 a stand-down (`silent`) / R2b a ping open there (hold 10 min), R3 too late;
  - R4 **the owner is on it** (their latest line in that chat is after the newest inbound line, or within 10 min before it);
  - R5 **settle** (hold until the newest source is 15 min old — the median read was 2.6 min);
  - R6 per kind (`unanswered`: silent in a live chat, held until 4 h old, then at most a note — read never silences it, only a reply does; a noticer `household` item with no deadline is a relay → silent; a `deadline`/`conflict` with no close is at most a note; `security` is exactly a note, never a push);
  - R7 not yet (hold until `closes_at − lead`: travel 3 h, household 1 h, conflict 3 h, follow-through 1 h, deadline 24 h);
  - R8 **one follow-up** (the same key or chat-kind-sources surfaced in 24 h with nothing new → silent);
  - R9 the channel — the owner's own words bind (`ceilings_from_owner`), while what the ladder learned from being ignored only rides along as `advice` for the speaker, never a block (design decision, 2026-10-11); `thread` only for a follow-through in a chat that addressed Edward within 7 days; `ambient` only for non-sensitive household;
  - R10 **precision first** (`EDWARD_NOTICE_PUSH=floor`, the default: only travel or household closing within 3 h may push; `gate` lifts it);
  - R11 **quiet hours** (`EDWARD_QUIET_HOURS`, local `start-end` hours, default `22-7`; the window wraps past midnight when start > end, equal hours mean none, an unparseable value falls back to 22–7; `speak_gate.quiet_hours()` reads it per call): a **travel** floor item that closes before quiet hours end still pushes — only care alerts and travel closing within 3 h may buzz at night (design decision, 2026-10-11) — so a household floor item is a note, with the household screen when it isn't sensitive (a review found a courier's gate-code text pushed at 23:10), and the templated fallback of R0 and R13 never pushes a household item at night either (`may_push_at`); an item closing more than 30 minutes after quiet hours end holds until they end; otherwise a note;
  - R12 the push budget only when `EDWARD_PUSH_BUDGET` is set (a note, plus `ambient` for household; by default there is no daily push limit);
  - R13 the speak budget (`EDWARD_SPEAK_BUDGET` 8 a day, one per chat an hour; a floor push becomes the fallback).

  The fixtures F1–F24 in `tests/test_speak_gate.py` are production cases from the original install.
- **The speaker** (`notice` mode): `ambient` → `expression_service.run_expression_pulse("notice", extra=render_ambient(...))`, else a note; a `fallback` → `reach_service.tell_owner(template, urgent=True, origin="fallback")` with only the sender, the time and the close ("A text from <sender> at 9:31 pm looks like it needs you before 10:00 pm — I couldn't check it in time."); `note` / `push` / `thread` → **`mind_service.wake("speak", …)`** in `edward-speak` with no history, on the mind route unless `EDWARD_SPEAK_MODEL` names another, `SPEAK_SYSTEM_PROMPT` (verify or end `DROP:`; prepare and hold the irreversible step; one offer in the grammar), the `render_speak_pack` (the claim, the decision and pushes left, every source in full with read state, 20 lines of each source chat, the owner's lines over 6 h, what was already said today, the digest, their standing instructions), a `TurnPolicy` from `speak_policy_kwargs` (`may_push` only for push, `may_reach_people` and `reply_to` only for a thread, with `final_text_is_reply` so a speaker that ends `DROP:` is never nudged into posting) and `legacy_speak_tools(channel)` for `EDWARD_TOOLSET=legacy`, inside `reach_service.speak_scope` so it can only lower. After it: a delivery → `spoken`; a send into the chat → `spoken`/`thread`; `DROP:` → `dropped`; nothing → `silent` ("speaker left it"); a failure → the fallback push when a floor item closes within 30 min, else one retry through the gate 10 min later (`failed`, `due_at`), then `failed`. Each speak turn writes a `speak` ledger row (the speak budget counts them). The presence and the stage never show what a speak wake noticed — only the fixed `wake_state.SPEAK_LINE`, "Something came up — I left a note."
- **Where the owner is reached** is `reach_service`'s, not the noticer's ([push-and-notes.md](push-and-notes.md)): the owner's channel (`owner_channel`, `EDWARD_OWNER_CHANNEL`) reads the owner's handles from `heartbeat_config.imessage_owner_handles`, then `EDWARD_OWNER_HANDLES`, and a link in a text points at `host_config.public_base_url()` — `EDWARD_PUBLIC_BASE_URL`, else the origin of `EDWARD_PUBLIC_SCREEN_URL`, else `http://localhost:3000`.
- **The sweep** (`noticing_service.sweep`, run by the `*/5` `proactive_sweep`; code only): an intention made in a ping (`source_ref.turn_source` `ping`/`ping_owner`, or a `ping_id`) whose horizon passed in the last day with no pending event carrying it, and not closed, becomes a `follow_through` noticing (`origin='sweep'`, keyed `intention:<id>`; `shadow`/`notice` only); **nothing is left `open`**: an `open` noticing untouched for `STRANDED_AFTER` (the speak bound plus margins, 6 min) that this process is not surfacing (`_SURFACING`) — a speak wake the process died under (a restart kills it), or a row whose gate never ran — becomes `failed`, due now while attempts remain (`_sweep_stranded`); a surface or a gate that raises does the same at once (`_after_surface_failure` mirrors the speaker's failure branch, the templated push included; a gate failure counts an attempt, so a gate that keeps raising gives up); held items and failed speaks that are due are re-gated with fresh facts; failed passes are retried once; outcomes are classified; ambient items past their close are expired; noticings older than 30 days are pruned once a day. It never raises.
- **Outcomes and the ladder**: `classify_outcome` (pure, first match): `rejected` (a "wrong call" on the speak wake's ledger row), `opened` (the push opened, or "right call"), `acted` (the owner's tapback or inline reply on Edward's message, read from chat.db; the dose logged after a care alert; the owner messaging the chat or a named person before it closed — **unless they were already in exchange there in the 30 min before**), `dismissed` (the note's "Done", `edward_notes.dismissed_at`), then once the window closed (`closes_at`, else 24 h) `seen` (a push followed within 10 min by any line of the owner's, or a confounded reply — neutral) or `ignored`. `speak_gate.channel_ceiling` folds a category's outcomes into the level the speaker is told about (advice, not a cap): travel and household start at push, security is pinned at note, the rest at note; a rejection lowers one level; two ignored *pushes* in a row lower push to note (ignored notes never demote — Today is read once a day); two dismissed notes lower note to journal; opened or acted raises one level; seven quiet days at a demoted level climb one level back toward where it was. The owner's own words do bind (`ceilings_from_owner`: "push me about travel" → push, "don't push me about work" → note, "leave family alone" → journal), read from their standing instructions. The levels, with probation dates, are in the health report's `categories` and in the code-written `digest` the nightly and the speaker read; care alerts never enter the ladder.
- **The eval** (`services/notice_eval.py`, `backend/scripts/notice_eval.py`): fifteen cases derived from the original install's real messages, rewritten with invented people and places. Must-notice: `flight_checkin`, `yard_service` (a service arriving in 20 min), `friend_waiting` (someone waiting only on the owner for 5 h), `lost_item_abroad`, `family_unanswered_4h`, `credential_in_owners_line`. Must-not-speak: `cash_in_live_chat`, `group_chatter`, `lunch_shelf`, `groceries_delivered`, `night_noise` (2:40 am), `owner_already_there`, `family_read` (a family text just read), `aimed_at_owner_60s` (a message 60 s old), `echo_of_edward`.
  - The runner uses the real noticer under `mind:notice_eval` (≈ $0.01) on a `*_test` database off :5432 with every send held, under process guards installed before anything runs (`services.eval_guards.install_for_eval`: osascript, `open` and Chrome refused, the live local services off limits, a scratch computer). `install_for_eval` takes only `EDWARD_LLM_PROVIDER` and `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` from this checkout's `.env` (then `EDWARD_LIVE_REPO`'s) when the shell has not set them, prints the provider, then installs the guards. Contacts is off and the speaker has no `lookup_contact` (it once ran `open -ga Contacts` and AppleScript against the owner's real address book), and `EDWARD_NOTICE_EVAL_TOOLSET=v2` (a shell, a browser) runs only with the guards on.
  - `--speak` also runs the speaker on three cases and on a planted misleading flight claim it must `DROP:` (≈ $0.4); since the speaker is a tracked task the runner waits for it (`noticing_service.drain`) before scoring and before the next case truncates its tables. The runner starts the LangGraph checkpointer (`initialize_graph`) as the judgement and ping checks do — without it every speak wake failed "Graph not initialized" and only the templated fallback spoke (the first real run, 2026-10-11).
  - **The speaker's world is the case's**: its turn runs on the case's clock (`_case_clock` points `streaming.now_local` at it), the chat.db readers at a path that does not exist (the first real run read real messages on the real clock), and **each case brings its own calendar, never the owner's** (`notice_eval.CALENDAR` — a holiday, an eye exam, a toll bill due tomorrow at noon; `Fixture.calendar` overrides), served through `calendar_service.read_between` under every calendar reader for that case only (`_case_calendar`); a case from a trip runs on that place's clock (`Fixture.tz`). Until 2026-10-11 every pack carried the owner's real calendar, so results moved with their week and it went out with each run.
  - The runner prints why each item was dropped (`notice_eval.pass_line`: "invalid 1: source_not_in_pack 1"). Its verdict is read where its prompt puts it, the closing line (`noticing_service.closing_drop`: the last `DROP:`/`LEFT:` in the flattened note), not `startswith("DROP:")`. The flight case also names its category (`expect_category="travel"`): a push needs that word, so the case fails and says what the noticer wrote when it differs.
  - **Run the eval with the production prompt before `EDWARD_PROACTIVITY` leaves `off`** — on the original install, after the drop fix, it passed 15/15 on three full runs and `family_unanswered_4h` 15/15 alone, with no dropped item in 57 noticer calls (without `--speak`). Exit 2 on a failed case: the flag stays where it is. Every complaint or praise from the owner about what Edward said becomes a new fixture (a manual step).
- **Observability**: `proactivity_report` (`GET /api/health/report` → `proactivity`; [ledgers.md](ledgers.md)) — a dead noticer reads `stalled`, a failing one `failing`.
